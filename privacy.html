Privacy

The short version: your calls are yours.

Talk2Me has no account system, no analytics, and no call history. This page explains exactly what leaves a phone, and to whom.

What stays on your phone

  • Audio. It is never recorded or uploaded.
  • Call history. None is kept; there is no database of past calls.
  • Contacts. The app never requests access to them.
  • TURN settings. URLs, username, and password are stored in the app's private SharedPreferences. They are not sent anywhere except to the relay you configured.
  • Room codes. Not stored after the call ends.

What leaves your phone

During a call, three kinds of traffic leave the device:

1. Signaling through a public MQTT broker

Phones announce themselves on a topic derived from the room code: talk2me/v1/ plus the first 40 hex characters of SHA-256(talk2me/v1/ + code). SDP offers/answers and ICE candidates are encrypted with AES-GCM using a PBKDF2-derived key from the same room code. The broker sees ciphertext, message sizes, and timing, but not the code or the contents.

The broker is one of the public test brokers HiveMQ, EMQX, or Mosquitto, connected over TLS. These are shared third-party services; their own privacy policies apply.

2. STUN requests

To discover a public address, the app may contact Google, Cloudflare, and Nextcloud STUN servers. A STUN request reveals your IP address to that server, as any internet connection does.

3. Media, direct or relayed

Audio is sent directly to the other participants using DTLS-SRTP. If you configure a TURN relay, the encrypted packets are forwarded by that provider. The relay can see IP addresses and traffic volume, but cannot decrypt the audio.

No TURN provider is built in. If you do not configure one, no relay is involved.

Permissions and why they exist

Microphone
Required to make a call. Requested when you start or join.
Notifications
Android 13+ only; shows the ongoing-call notification with Mute and End.
Internet and network state
Signaling, STUN, and peer-to-peer audio.
Foreground service (microphone)
Keeps the call alive with the screen locked.
Wake lock
Keeps the CPU and Wi-Fi awake for the duration of the call.
Bluetooth connect
Android 12+ only; lets the system route audio to a headset.

The app does not request camera, location, contacts, storage, or telephony permissions.

No tracking, no ads, no accounts

There is no sign-up, no user identifier, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK. The app contains no analytics calls. Crash logs stay on the device unless you choose to share them in a bug report.

Because there is no account, there is nothing to delete on a server. Uninstalling the app removes its local settings.

This website

This site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no external fonts, scripts, or images. All assets are served from the same origin. The only outbound links are to GitHub, the documentation references, and TURN providers.

Questions

The source code is public at github.com/lawalTheWest/talk2me. For privacy questions or corrections, open an issue there.
